Welcome to Rent Saned

Rent the medical equipment you need, for as long as you need it, without paying the full price.

The Idea | Benefits | How It Works | Rental Prices

The Idea

When someone needs medical equipment for a short period of time, he can rent it instead of buying it.

The Problem

When you buy equipment at full price, it costs a lot of money, even though you do not need it for a lifetime.

Our Solution

Rent the equipment until you finish, then return it, all for a low price!

Benefits

  • Save money
  • No storage problem after you recover.
  • All equipment is clean and checked
  • Free delivery to your home.

How It Works

  1. Create an account.
  2. Choose the equipment you need:
    • Mobility: wheelchair, crutches, walker
    • Home care: hospital bed
  3. We deliver it to your door.
  4. When you finish, we pick it up
