Our Mission & Vision

Patients recovering from an accident or surgery often require assistive rehabilitation hardware, such as manual wheelchairs, hospital beds, knee walkers, and crutches, for short intervals spanning four to eight weeks. Purchasing these devices outright imposes unnecessary financial strain on families, with costs frequently exceeding thousands of riyals for equipment destined to sit unused once recovery is complete.

Rent Saned addresses these challenges through a centralized web platform that provides flexible weekly and monthly rentals for patients in need of certified, sanitized rehabilitation equipment in Jeddah.

Our Service Standards

  1. Inventory Management:
    • Mobility Aids: Wheelchairs, knee walkers, and forearm crutches.
    • Home Care Hardware: Adjustable hospital beds and pressure-relief mattresses.
  2. Clinical Safety Protocols:
    • Hospital-grade multi-stage sterilization between rental cycles.
    • Structural safety checks prior to every customer delivery.
  3. Flexible Rental Terms:
    • Weekly and monthly pricing tiers.
    • Transparent deposit refunds upon equipment retrieval.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How does the sanitation process work?

Every piece of returned hardware is disinfected using clinical-grade sanitizing agents, tested for full structural integrity, and sealed in protective wrap prior to dispatch.

How is the security deposit returned?

Once the rental period concludes, our field representative inspects the item during pickup, and the refundable deposit is credited back to your account immediately.
