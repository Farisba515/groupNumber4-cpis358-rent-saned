About Rent Saned

Our Mission & Vision

Many people need medical equipment after an accident or a surgery, but only for a short time, usually four to eight weeks. Buying a wheelchair or a hospital bed costs a lot of money, and after the patient gets better, it just stays at home without use.

Rent Saned helps these patients and their families. You can rent the equipment you need by the day, the week or the month, and we deliver it to your home in Jeddah.

What We Offer

  1. Our Equipment:
    • Mobility: wheelchairs, crutches and walkers.
    • Home care: hospital beds.
  2. Clean and Safe:
    • We clean every item after each rental.
    • We check every item before we deliver it.
  3. Easy Rental:
    • Rent by the day, the week or the month.
    • You get your deposit back when you return the item.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is the equipment clean?

Yes. We clean every item when it comes back to us, and we check that it works well before we send it to the next customer.

How do I get my deposit back?

When you finish, we pick up the item from your home and check it. Then we give you back your 100 SAR deposit.
